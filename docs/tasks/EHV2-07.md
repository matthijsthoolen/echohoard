# EHV2-07 feature — Integrated V2 acceptance and recovery

> V2 tracking contract. Execute [`docs/stories/EHV2-07.md`](../stories/EHV2-07.md).

## Outcome

Prove the V2 capabilities compose safely at scale and in the private environment before declaring the initiative complete.

## Start state

Each V2 feature has focused evidence, but cross-feature policy, convergence, deployment, and recovery are not yet accepted.

## End state

Synthetic and attended private evidence proves multi-account live-to-backup convergence, preserved deletion events, merge/unmerge, privacy, soft deletion, media recovery, transcription, restart/reconnect, backup, and isolated restore.

## Acceptance criteria

- Cross-feature negative tests show no visibility, account, archive, or MCP leakage.
- Replayed/out-of-order live and backup inputs converge after merge, exclusion, deletion, and media recovery operations.
- Private deployment recovers from sidecar/web/worker restarts without duplicate or lost acknowledged observations.
- An isolated restore reproduces accepted normalized state, policies, transcripts, and CAS references; linked-device re-pairing is documented separately from archive recovery.

## Verification

Generated-scale integration suite, browser/MCP policy matrix, private deployment checklist, attended linked-device/LiteLLM acceptance, and isolated restore.

## Out of scope

New product behavior, production bulk purge, WhatsApp writes, or accepting unresolved required evidence.

EHV2-01, EHV2-02, EHV2-03, EHV2-04, EHV2-05, and EHV2-06.
