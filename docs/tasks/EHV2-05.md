# EHV2-05 feature — Media galleries and recovery

> V2 tracking contract. Execute [`docs/stories/EHV2-05.md`](../stories/EHV2-05.md).

## Outcome

Provide WhatsApp-like per-chat and global media browsing, make missing media explicit, and safely recover bytes from owner-selected directories or uploads.

## Start state

Attachments have CAS storage, safe delivery, message links, and availability states, but no gallery or recovery workflow.

## End state

Bounded galleries filter media by type/status/date/chat; missing items show why they are unavailable. Recovery sources are staged safely, exact hashes link automatically, and all metadata/fuzzy candidates require owner confirmation.

## Acceptance criteria

- Galleries never load an unbounded chat or expose hidden/locked/deleted/MCP-denied content outside its policy context.
- Available, missing, unsafe, unresolved, and recovered states are distinct and accessible.
- Directory scanning is limited to configured recovery roots; uploads are size/type bounded and quarantined before hashing.
- Ambiguous candidates never auto-link; accepted bytes enter CAS atomically and retain recovery provenance.

## Verification

Query/performance tests, browser gallery tests, filesystem/path/upload hostility tests, matching fixtures, and recovery audit tests.

## Out of scope

Immich physical ownership, arbitrary host paths, OCR/image understanding, or deleting source recovery files.
