# EHV2-09 feature — Privacy-safe unified reads

> V2 post-review remediation tracker. Execute [`docs/stories/EHV2-09.md`](../stories/EHV2-09.md).

## Outcome

Make grouping, conversation summaries, statistics, and pagination obey unified-conversation semantics and privacy policy without metadata leaks or skipped records.

## Start state

Grouping endpoints can enumerate protected sources, merged conversation lists can duplicate chats, source-account statistics can include other accounts, and several cursors do not match their query ordering or context.

## End state

Grouping is policy-authorized and non-enumerating, merged chats list once with complete summaries, source filters scope every aggregate, and every cursor is stable and bound to its exact read context.

## Acceptance criteria

- Hidden/locked grouping metadata and commands require the same authorization as content reads.
- Merge/unmerge changes presentation lists and summaries immediately without duplicate chats.
- Source-account filters scope messages, people, media, and activity consistently.
- Forward/backward pagination neither skips nor duplicates tied, null, or multiply-linked records, and cursors cannot be replayed across contexts.

## Verification

PostgreSQL policy, merge/unmerge, aggregate, and cursor permutation tests plus route-level non-enumeration checks.

## Out of scope

Privacy mutation UX, MCP disclosure policy changes, automatic merge suggestions, or offset pagination.

## Blocked by

EHV2-02-02 and EHV2-03-03.
